# Posto app surface

Mode: Operate. Daily check-in tool: Today feed, Tracker, Insights, My situation, Settings. Brand token: Posto (Italian for place, seat, job). Name chosen by the user; availability not yet checked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a customs hall. Every job is a visa page and every verdict is an inked rubber stamp with its conditions of entry; refuses the neutral card-feed dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Cobalt desk (#2B35FF field, the loud committed color) under pale lilac-white visa pages with fine blue guilloche lines. Stamp inks: green FIT, orange CHECK, red NOT FOR YOU, violet for customs marks. Bricolage Grotesque for headings and UI, Courier Prime for typed fields and the machine-readable zone. Recognizable with content removed: cobalt field, guilloche page, tilted double-ruled stamp.
STORY: A tired job seeker opens it and instantly sees which pages got a green stamp, reads the conditions in plain words, and saves or skips with one stamp-press.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt desk. Top strip: tilted POSTO wordmark stamp left, section tabs like passport page edges, user right. Below, a two-column spread of visa pages; each page has a header band (source, age), the job title at 28px, company and place in typed Courier, a large rotated stamp at upper right, conditions list with + ! x marks, a two-line MRZ at the foot, and three stamp-style buttons. Primary action SAVE sits bottom-left of each page.
FORM: Passport and visa stamp world (my own pick, candidate 1 of my list), seed key 4d229e4c; assigned challenger set ignored because the user asked for funky and delegated the choice.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
