# Tailoring rules

Adapted from the application method in ai-job-search (Mads Lorentzen, MIT), via anyjobsearch.

## CV

One base CV, one tailored variant per posting.

- One page. If it does not fit, the weakest bullet goes, not the font size.
- Lead with evidence: live products, repositories with green CI, shipped work. A repo with tests proves more than an adjective.
- Mirror the posting's vocabulary only where it is true. If the posting says a tool the candidate has never used, the CV says
  nothing about it; the note to the candidate mentions the gap instead.
- Reframe, never invent. "Contact-centre QA" can honestly become "multi-channel quality assurance and root-cause analysis";
  it cannot become "software QA at scale".
- Keep dates, grades and numbers exact. Reviewers cross-check.
- Single column, standard section names (Experience, Education, Skills), real text only, so parsers read it without guessing.

## Cover letter

Only where the posting expects one. Why this company (one specific, true observation), why this candidate (the two strongest
evidence matches), what happens next. Under 200 words, plain sentences, no em dashes, no "I am passionate about". Say what the
candidate would do in the role, not a recap of the CV.

## Gaps

For each thing the posting asks for that the CV does not show, say whether it is a real gap or only missing from the wording,
then give three concrete edits.
